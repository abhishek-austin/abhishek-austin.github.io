Privacy Policy

This policy covers how ANB Solutions, Inc. ("we," "us") handles personal information from this website, from the apps and services we offer, and from client engagements. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

This website

If you use the contact form, we collect your name, email, and message, and use them only to reply. We use Google Analytics to understand site usage, which sets cookies you can block in your browser. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, loads fonts from Google Fonts, and sends form submissions through Formspree.

Apps and services

Using any app or service we offer is optional and opt-in. You choose whether to create an account, grant a device permission, or share content. We collect only what you provide and what the app needs to work, such as account details, content you choose to submit, and basic usage and crash data, and we use it only to provide and improve the app. Some features may use third-party AI or cloud providers to process content you submit. We do not use your content to train our own models without your consent. You can stop using an app, revoke permissions, or ask us to delete your account and data at any time. An app may show you additional notices, which add to this policy.

Client engagements

When we access a client's systems during an advisory engagement, we do so on the client's own accounts and only as needed for the work. That data is governed by our engagement agreement and any Data Processing Addendum, not by this policy.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your information. Our website and apps are not directed to children under 13. To make a request or ask a question, email abhishek@theanbgroup.com.

We may update this policy, and the date above shows the latest version. See also our Terms of Use.
