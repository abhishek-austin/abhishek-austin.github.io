Privacy Policy

How ANB Solutions, Inc. handles information from this website, from the apps and services we offer, and from client advisory engagements.

This policy explains what personal information ANB Solutions, Inc. ("ANB Solutions," "we," "us") collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. ANB Solutions is a technology practice based in Austin, Texas, operating at theanbgroup.com and its subdomains.

It covers three things, each handled a little differently:

  • Part A: this website and its contact form.
  • Part B: apps, tools, and online services that ANB Solutions builds and offers.
  • Part C: client data we access during advisory engagements.

The sections on your rights, security, and contact at the end apply to all three. Use of this website is also governed by our Terms of Use.

Part A. This website

What we collect

  • Contact form. When you use the contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message you provide, and use them only to respond to your inquiry and follow up about a possible engagement.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. It sets cookies and collects information such as approximate location, device and browser type, and pages viewed. We do not use it to identify you personally.
  • Delivery data. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Loading a page shares your IP address and basic request information with these providers so the page can be delivered.

Cookies

The site itself does not need cookies to work. Google Analytics sets cookies for usage measurement, and you can block or delete them in your browser at any time.

Providers for this website

  • Formspree, which delivers contact form submissions to us.
  • Google Analytics, for website usage measurement.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces used on the site.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts and serves the site.

Part B. Apps and services

ANB Solutions builds and offers apps, tools, and online services from time to time, on the web and on mobile app stores. This part applies to all of them unless a specific app shows you its own privacy notice, in which case that notice adds to this one.

Use is opt-in

Using any of our apps is your choice. You decide whether to install an app, create an account, sign in, grant a device permission, or share any content with it. Features that need data you have not shared simply stay off, and you can stop using an app, revoke permissions, or delete your account at any time.

What we may collect

  • Information you provide. Account details such as your name and email address, the content you choose to enter or upload, and your preferences.
  • Information from sign-in providers. If you choose to sign in with a provider such as Apple or Google, we receive the basic profile information you authorize, such as your name and email address.
  • Information from device permissions. Only if you grant it, for example access to your camera, photos, or notifications. You can turn any permission off in your device settings.
  • Usage and diagnostic information. Device type, operating system and app version, crash reports, and basic usage analytics that help us keep the app working and improve it.

How we use it

  • To provide the features you choose to use and to keep your account working.
  • To maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve our apps.
  • To communicate with you about your account or about changes to the app.

AI features

Some apps include features powered by third-party AI services. When you use one of those features, the content you submit for it is sent to that service to generate the result. We do not use your content to train our own models unless you separately and explicitly agree.

Payments

If an app offers purchases or subscriptions, payments are handled by the app store or a payment processor. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or use it for third-party advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run our apps, such as hosting, storage, analytics, crash reporting, and AI providers, each limited to what its function requires. We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect the rights and safety of our users or others, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case this policy continues to apply to it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep app data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the app. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time, from within the app where that option is offered, or by emailing abhishek@theanbgroup.com. We delete it within a reasonable period, except where we need to keep certain records to meet legal obligations.

Part C. Client data during advisory engagements

In advisory work, architecture and security reviews, and SOC 2 and compliance readiness work, we may access a client's systems and data. When we do:

  • We access that data on the client's own cloud accounts and enterprise subscriptions, under the client's control, only as needed for the engagement.
  • We act as a service provider or processor on the client's behalf. Handling of that data is governed by the engagement agreement and, where applicable, a Data Processing Addendum, not by this policy.
  • We do not use client data for any purpose beyond the engagement, and we return or remove access when the engagement ends.

Prospective clients who need a Data Processing Addendum or want to review our data-handling terms can contact us.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on your consent (for example, when you opt in to an app feature or non-essential cookies), on performance of a contract (to provide an app or service you signed up for), and on our legitimate interest in responding to inquiries and keeping our website and apps secure and working. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to receive a copy of the data you gave us. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them. To make a request, email abhishek@theanbgroup.com. We will respond within the time the law requires.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information in our care, including least-privilege access, encryption in transit, and prompt removal of access that is no longer needed. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

International transfers

We and our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Children

This website and our apps are not directed to children under 13, or under 16 where local law sets a higher age, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices, apps, or the law change. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we make a material change to how an app uses your data, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email abhishek@theanbgroup.com. ANB Solutions, Inc., Austin, Texas.
